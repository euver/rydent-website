About us

About RYDENT

Building safer and smarter vehicle transactions

RYDENT is a mobile app that helps buyers and sellers of secondhand motorcycles and cars make safer, more confident deals through vehicle verification and stolen-vehicle alerts.

A student startup project of Davao del Norte State College, built by team FRAVEX in Panabo City, Davao del Norte. In October 2026 we tested the prototype with 20 potential customers and used their feedback to improve it.

◎

Our Mission

To make buying and selling secondhand vehicles safer and more transparent, with built-in verification and stolen-vehicle alerts.

◉

Our Vision

A future where every secondhand vehicle deal is built on trust, so buyers, sellers and owners can deal with confidence.

“A good deal feels better when it’s a clean one.”

The problem

Buyers can’t tell if a vehicle is stolen before they pay.

They rely on the seller’s word, and stolen-vehicle reports never reach them.

01 The Pains

  • Buyers depend heavily on the seller’s word and documents.
  • Stolen vehicles may be advertised without the buyer knowing.
  • Vehicle details and theft reports are scattered across sources.
  • Checking a vehicle takes extra time and effort.

02 The Gaps

  • Marketplaces connect people but rarely verify vehicles.
  • Theft reports are shared on social media and may never reach buyers.
  • Buyers need an organized alert when a vehicle matches a report.
  • Owners need one clear place to report a stolen vehicle.
Features

Our Prototype

Vehicle Marketplace

Buy and sell motorcycles, cars, trucks and vans in one place.

Vehicle Verification

At the viewing, enter the plate, chassis or engine number. The app compares them with reported stolen-vehicle records and shows a clear result.

Private Information

Plate, chassis, engine and OR/CR details are never shown on listings.

Sell a Vehicle

List with photos, price, mileage and details in minutes.

Verified Sellers

Sellers upload an ID and a selfie. Buyers see a verified badge.

Stolen Vehicle Reporting

Owners report a stolen vehicle in three steps and track each report.

Stolen Vehicle Alerts

If a vehicle you’re checking matches a verified stolen-vehicle report, you get a red “Do not proceed” alert with what to do next.

Messaging

Chat with the seller to confirm details and set a viewing.

Process

How it works

1

Find

Search by make, model or city and open a listing to see price, mileage and seller.

2

Check

Enter the plate, chassis and engine numbers at the viewing.

3

Get the result

“No Matching Report Found” or a red “Do not proceed” warning.

4

Connect

Check the seller’s profile and ID badge, then chat to arrange the viewing.
