About RYDENT

Building safer and smarter vehicle transactions

RYDENT is a mobile app that helps buyers and sellers of secondhand motorcycles and cars make safer and more confident transactions through vehicle verification and stolen-vehicle alerts.

RYDENT is a student startup project of Davao del Norte State College, built by team FRAVEX in Panabo City, Davao del Norte. In October 2026 we tested the prototype with 20 potential customers and used their feedback to improve it.

Buyers can't tell if a vehicle is stolen before they pay.

They rely on the seller's word, and stolen-vehicle reports never reach them.

01 The Pains

  • Buyers can't easily tell if a secondhand vehicle is stolen, and depend heavily on the seller's word and documents.
  • Some stolen vehicles may be advertised for sale without the buyer knowing.
  • Vehicle details and stolen-vehicle reports are scattered across different sources.
  • Checking a vehicle takes extra time and effort before a buyer can feel confident.

02 The Gaps

  • Existing marketplaces mainly focus on connecting buyers and sellers.
  • There is a need for a marketplace with a dedicated vehicle verification process.
  • Stolen-vehicle reports are often shared through social media and may not reach potential buyers.
  • Buyers need an organized way to receive alerts when a vehicle matches a stolen-vehicle report.
◎

Our Mission

To make buying and selling secondhand vehicles safer and more transparent by combining a vehicle marketplace with built-in verification and stolen-vehicle alerts.

◉

Our Vision

A future where every secondhand vehicle transaction is built on trust, so buyers, sellers and owners can deal with confidence.

Our Objectives

  • 1Give buyers and sellers one marketplace for secondhand motorcycles and cars.
  • 2Let buyers verify a vehicle at the viewing using its plate, chassis or engine number.
  • 3Warn buyers when a vehicle matches a verified stolen-vehicle report.
  • 4Give vehicle owners an organized way to report a stolen vehicle.

Features

Our Prototype

Vehicle Marketplace

Buy and sell secondhand motorcycles and cars in one platform. Browse by type: motorcycles, cars, trucks and vans.

Vehicle Verification

Verify a vehicle upon viewing by entering available identifying information such as the plate, chassis or engine number.

Private Information

Plate, chassis and engine numbers and OR/CR details are never displayed publicly on seller listings.

Stolen Vehicle Reporting

Vehicle owners can report their stolen vehicles through the app and track the status of each report.

Stolen Vehicle Alerts

If a vehicle being checked matches a verified stolen-vehicle report, the buyer receives an alert.

Sell a Vehicle

List a motorcycle or car with photos, price, mileage and details. Plate, chassis and engine numbers stay private.

Verified Sellers

Sellers upload an ID and a selfie for review. Buyers see a verified badge on the seller profile.

Messaging

Ask about availability or arrange a viewing by chatting with the seller inside the app.

Process

How it works

1

Find

Search by make, model or city, or browse motorcycles, cars, trucks and vans. Open a listing to see its price, mileage and seller.

2

Check

At the viewing, enter the plate, chassis and engine numbers from the vehicle and its documents. The app compares them with reported stolen-vehicle records.

3

Get the result

No match shows “No Matching Report Found”. A match with a verified report shows a red “Do not proceed” warning with what to do next.

4

Connect

Check the seller's profile and ID badge, then send an inquiry and chat to arrange the viewing.

For sellers

List a vehicle, then verify your ID with a photo of your license and a selfie. A verified badge helps buyers trust you.

For owners

Report a stolen vehicle in three steps, get a report reference, and follow its status (submitted, under review, verified, recovered) in My Reports.
